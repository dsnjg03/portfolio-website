### The Road to Backend Mastery

While I have knowledge of building backends with Node.js, I wanted to expand my server-side stack. Since PHP still powers a massive portion of the web, and understanding it is crucial for a well-rounded backend developer, I chose to start learning it.

#### My Learning Strategy:
I am specifically focusing on **Native PHP (8.4)** rather than jumping straight into a framework in order to familiarize myself with PHP's syntax and object-oriented capabilities.

Furthermore, I am learning how to securely connect to **MySQL** databases using **PDO** and preventing SQL injection through prepared statements.

#### What's Next?
Before I allow myself to move onto **Laravel**, my goal is to master the fundamentals of PHP. This would mean comfortably building a native, barebones **REST API** to handle user authentication and basic CRUD operations. This will ensure I understand the *under the hood* mechanics of PHP.
